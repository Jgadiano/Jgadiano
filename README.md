## 👩‍💼 About Me

I'm a **Project Manager, Marketing Consultant, and Virtual Assistant** focused on helping businesses turn ideas into organized, efficient, and results-driven operations.

My experience spans **project management, marketing, customer experience, digital operations, website management, SEO, and client support**. I enjoy bringing structure to complex projects, improving workflows, coordinating teams, and finding practical ways to help businesses operate and grow more effectively.

I work with businesses and teams that need someone who can see both the **big picture and the details** — from planning and execution to communication, customer experience, and continuous improvement.

- 📋 Managing projects from planning through execution
- 📈 Developing and supporting digital marketing strategies
- 🤝 Improving customer experience and support processes
- ⚙️ Streamlining workflows and day-to-day operations
- 👥 Coordinating teams, tasks, priorities, and deadlines
- 🔍 Supporting SEO and online visibility initiatives
- 🌐 Managing website content and digital platforms
- 💬 Handling client communication and stakeholder coordination
- 📊 Tracking performance, progress, and opportunities for improvement
